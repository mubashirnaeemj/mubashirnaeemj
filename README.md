### 🚀 What I build

I build production-ready AI automation systems that streamline business operations through LLMs, backend engineering, workflow automation, and voice AI. My focus is scalable, reliable systems that solve real business problems, not flashy demos.

Currently building AI automation infrastructure at **Axioware** (since January 2026), where I built the dispatch, webhook and database layers of an AI calling platform that has placed **5,400+ Salesforce-driven calls to 1,500+ leads** (peak of 1,275 calls in a single day), plus a five-flow n8n SMS and voice system and an LLM lead-enrichment pipeline.

- 🔧 I care more about a system staying up at 3am than about a flashy prototype
- 🧩 Most of my work sits at the intersection of LLMs, backend engineering, and CRM/API integration
- 📈 Before automating anything, I spend time understanding the actual business process I'm replacing
- 🎓 BS in Artificial Intelligence (SMIU, 2026), now building production automation systems full-time at Axioware
